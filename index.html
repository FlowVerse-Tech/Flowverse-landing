The Reality Today

Appointments shouldn't live in a dozen places.

Local service booking is still fragmented across phone calls, lost chats, and missed direct messages. Both customers and parlour owners deserve a cleaner, simpler flow.

Fragmented Discovery

Customers cannot easily compare nearby service providers, detailed service menus, prices, and available time slots in one trusted place.

Digital Overhead

Small businesses rarely have the time, budget, or technical staff needed to build and maintain a custom website and search-friendly local presence.

Scattered Enquiries

Booking messages scattered across quick calls and chat apps get missed during peak hours, accidentally duplicated, or answered too late.

A note on context: India has a very large small-business ecosystem — more than 7.83 crore enterprises registered on the Udyam platforms as of 28 February 2026, according to the Ministry of MSME. This is shared solely to illustrate the vitality of local enterprise, not as a measure of TimeToU's own addressable market.

Focused Adoption

Starting with Chennai's vibrant salon community.

We are deliberately focusing our early efforts on Chennai to create deep value for local salons before expanding to other sectors.

  • Independent Salons & Beauty Parlours: Neighborhood grooming spaces looking for clean digital visibility and organized booking.
  • Independent Stylists & Therapists: Solo personal care professionals who want a reliable booking link without website overhead.
  • Multi-Outlet Local Brands: Boutique chains operating across Chennai zones that need consistent appointment management.

Why Chennai first?

We believe in starting deep rather than wide. By walking into Chennai parlours, listening to salon owners across Adyar, Anna Nagar, T. Nagar, Velachery, and OMR, and understanding real day-to-day booking friction, we ensure TimeToU solves practical problems.

As onboarding is proven in Chennai, we will gradually expand into adjacent personal-care services and neighboring regions.
